# Home Page

Welcome the homepage. This is a resume about me.

## About Me

Hello, I'm **Tavares**. I'm a second-year Canadian graphic design student at Humber **Polytechnic** and an artist for *11 years*.

Notably, I was one of the former 2025 club leaders for the **APECS** animation club at **St. Edmund Campion CSS**- with our hit animation, *Fishman Requiem*, getting 2nd place at the *2024 Vaughan Film Festival.* I was credited as playing and voice acting the character "Yellow Jacket". During my tenure as a club lead, I was also head of the club's storyboarding division.

Additionally, I have volunteered at the *Peel Guyanese Association* for the annual *Move* event to raise money for the *Peel Alzheimer's Society*, as well as an assistant in the *All 'Bout Ball Basketball Clinic*- helping students between the ages of 4 and 15 learn and understand the fundamentals of Basketball, improving on being a good team player and the importance of stretching before and after playing.

Now that we have that covered...
